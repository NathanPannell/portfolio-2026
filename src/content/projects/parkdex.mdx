## Why it exists

Parkdex began with one goal: visit every provincial park on Vancouver Island. I needed a better way to keep track of them and decide where to go next.

## What I built

Parkdex is a phone app for 122 national, provincial, and regional parks across Vancouver Island. Tap a pin to see the badge you'll earn and how long the drive takes. The map works offline, so it still works where there is no signal.

When you step inside a park's boundary, Kip the oystercatcher lets you know you made it. Check in and snap a photo to claim the badge. Your visits and photos stay on your phone.

There are 20 badges to fill your case with, from common to legendary, and each one can be shared to your story. Parkdex is in beta on Android, with an App Store waitlist.

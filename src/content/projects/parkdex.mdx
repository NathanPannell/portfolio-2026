## Why it exists

Parkdex began with one goal: visit all 117 provincial parks on Vancouver Island. I needed a better way to keep track of them and decide where to go next.

## What I built

The web app maps roughly 200 national, provincial, regional, and island parks. I built profiles, search, wishlists, visit tracking, completion progress, and badges around one practical question: where should I go next?

We walked into the hackathon without a project idea. Ninety minutes later, Paurav, Christopher, and I settled on “Doxx yourself for money” and had six hours to make it real.

I built the tracking backend while the team turned the premise into a working parody product. Doxxme lets someone publicly share their live location and browser history for crypto payment. It is a joke, but it is also a complete, live demo of the joke.

The project took first place in both the Solana and Convex tracks. The most useful part of the weekend was watching agentic coding handle OAuth setup, cloud database changes, and browser testing as one continuous workflow.

We arrived without a project idea. Ninety minutes later, Paurav, Christopher, and I had settled on “Doxx yourself for money” and had six hours left to make it real.

## What I owned

I built the tracking backend while the team turned the premise into a working parody product. Doxxme lets someone publicly share their live location and browser history in exchange for crypto.

## Result

We took first place in both the Solana and Convex tracks. The most useful lesson was seeing one agentic workflow carry OAuth setup, cloud database changes, and browser testing from start to finish.
